# Accessibility in visual output

Applies to every chart, table, diagram, dashboard, UI and slide.

- **Never encode meaning in color alone.** Every state, series or category also gets a label,
  shape, pattern, icon or a clear lightness difference.
- **Prefer blue and orange** for two-way contrasts (good/bad, before/after). Avoid red versus
  green as the only signal.
- **Label directly.** Put labels on lines and bars instead of relying on a legend matched by
  color.
- **Check contrast.** Text meets WCAG AA (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI
  parts).
- **Keep light and dark mode working,** and keep layouts usable at phone width.
- **Status words beat status colors:** "Passed", "Failed", "Stale" in the cell, not just a
  colored dot.
