# /money

Revenue analysis framework that surfaces monetization opportunities and diagnoses system issues.

## Usage

```
/money
/money [domain]
```

## Process

### Step 1: Deep Vault Scan

```bash
obsidian search:orphans
obsidian search:deadends
obsidian tags counts sort=count
obsidian daily:read
obsidian search:context query="revenue"
obsidian search:context query="client"
obsidian search:context query="pricing"
obsidian search:context query="income"
```

Extract: client work, revenue discussions, skills, systems, gaps, ideas from 30 days of daily notes.

### Step 2: Asset Inventory

Catalog with evidence: skills, relationships, intellectual property, infrastructure, audience, credibility.

### Step 3: Revenue Diagnostics

Analyze: attention-to-revenue conversion, revenue type distribution, sales infrastructure, pricing structure, product-to-service ratio.

### Step 4: Beyond the Vault

Examine: assumptions about money, market context, packaging gaps, competitive positioning.

### Step 5: Revenue Opportunities

Generate across 8 categories:
1. Services (new offerings from existing skills)
2. Products (packageable knowledge)
3. Low-hanging fruit (can start this week)
4. Medium-term (1-3 months setup)
5. Long-term (6-12 months development)
6. Undermonetized assets (things already built, not earning)
7. Pricing corrections (currently underpriced)
8. Network/equity paths (relationship-based opportunities)

### Step 6: Temporal Tracking

Check previous /money runs for evolution.

### Step 7: Anti-Patterns

Reject: generic suggestions, constraint violations, unrealistic scaling, vague opportunities.

### Step 8: Prioritization

- Top 5 by effort-to-revenue ratio
- Immediate play (can start this week)
- Biggest upside (highest potential)
- Surprising opportunity (non-obvious)
- Structural fix (systemic improvement)

### Step 9: Actionable Builds

Provide concrete deliverables: service offerings, sponsorship decks, outreach templates, pricing pages, pitch documents.

## Principles

- Diagnose before suggesting.
- Be direct about structural problems.
- Include beyond-vault opportunities.
- Account for life constraints.
